Draw on your Mac with your iPad and Apple Pencil.

UniversePen shows your Mac screen on the iPad and sends every Pencil stroke back as real tablet input – with pressure, tilt, rotation (Pencil Pro), hover and squeeze. Drawing apps on the Mac see a pen tablet, not a mouse.

PEN DISPLAY
• Mac screen, a single window or a section on the iPad, sharp at full resolution
• Pressure curve per app, smoothing, tip threshold
• Instant local ink so strokes appear without waiting for the picture
• Radial menu on squeeze, shortcut bar, undo by two-finger scrub
• Ruler for straight lines, reference photo to draw from, local zoom
• Laser pointer for presenting, full-resolution screenshots, clipboard copy

PEN TABLET MODE
• Black surface with a dot grid and an adjustable active area – like a screenless pen tablet, great for rhythm games
• Presets per app, rotation, freely placed keys on the screen (hold several at once)

MADE FOR YOU
• Profiles, first-run setup with only the features you want, 7 languages
• Works with Pencils without hover too (double-tap to click, or aim with the finger)
• Several iPads: an extra iPad becomes an input tablet

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• iPad and Mac talk only to each other – Wi-Fi or USB cable, encrypted, paired by code
• No account, no server, no analytics

REQUIREMENTS
UniversePen needs the free companion app "UniversePen Host" on a Mac with macOS 15 or later (download: github.com/bedsheetsosu/universepen). Without a Mac you can try everything in the built-in demo.

UniversePen is open source (GPL-3.0).
